# Design System — The Pink Standard

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Design Direction & Thesis

"The Pink Standard" reclaims pink not as delicate ornament, but as unapologetic authority, high-fashion editorial majesty, and radiant luxury. Rooted in contemporary haute couture art direction, Swiss typographic precision, and real-time WebGL rendering physics.

## Palette Architecture (Pink With Depth)

| Token | Hex | Role | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blush | `#FCE4EC` | Soft Luminous Field | Editorial headlines, soft accents |
| Powder Pink | `#F4B8CD` | Secondary Surface Tint | Subheadings, badges, border highlights |
| Rose | `#E88BAF` | Chromatic Accent | Micro-labels, active states, timeline nodes |
| Hot Pink | `#F0529B` | High-Frequency Primary | Interactive CTAs, hover glows, live beacons |
| Deep Raspberry | `#B83270` | Dramatic Contrast | Gradient stops, active cards, badge frames |
| Burgundy | `#591D3B` | Structural Depth | Glassmorphic containers, card surfaces |
| Soft Ivory | `#FFF9F7` | Primary Foreground | Body typography, high-contrast display text |
| Near Black | `#21171D` | Deep Ground | Page ground, atmospheric backdrop |

## Typography Stack

- **Editorial Serif**: `Cormorant Garamond` & `Playfair Display` (Oversized display headlines, monumental titles, italic manifesto excerpts)
- **Contemporary Sans**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Clean body text, high legibility, measure 65–75ch)
- **Display Accent**: `Syne` (Section sub-headers, high-impact numbers)
- **Monospace & Metadata**: `Space Grotesk` (Labels, timeline dates, telemetry tags, coordinates)

## Motion & Tactile Physics

- **WebGL Atmosphere**: Originkit `GlimmerVale` heightfield shader with iridescent glitter ribbons and ambient bloom.
- **Micro-Interactions**: Exponential ease-out (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), 400ms surface transitions, scale(1.05) on interactive cards.
- **Feedback Loops**: Confetti bursts upon email copy and inquiry dispatch, sensory audio simulation toggle.

## Core Component Scaffolding

1. **Editorial Floating Header**: Glassmorphic `#21171D/85` container, active section indicators, quick CV download pill.
2. **Atmospheric Hero**: Layered WebGL backdrop behind oversized serif "ANJALI", availability pill, dual primary CTAs, live metrics.
3. **Magazine About Spread**: Asymmetrical dual-column composition, monochrome-to-color portrait hover, manifesto cards.
4. **Curated Work Showcase**: Art-directed project viewports with in-depth architecture modal.
5. **Career Anthology Ledger**: Interactive chronological ledger with measurable impact highlights.
6. **Discipline Matrix**: Typography-first skill categorizations (no arbitrary percentage bars).
7. **Pedigree & Honors**: Academic distinction alongside global awards (Awwwards, Red Dot, FWA).
8. **The Curated Mind**: Personal aesthetic laws, studio rituals, signature quotes.
9. **The Closing Invitation**: High-impact closing headline, one-click email copy, social matrix, and print/export CV sheet.
