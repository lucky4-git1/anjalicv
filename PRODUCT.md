# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, Originkit WebGL (Glimmer Vale).

## Users

Design leaders, creative directors, luxury brand executives, hiring managers, and collaborators seeking world-class creative leadership, digital experience design, and high-craft brand technology.

## Product Purpose

An unforgettable, Awwwards-worthy personal CV and portfolio for Anjali—a digital experience that establishes her as an elite creative director and experience designer through high-fashion editorial art direction, expressive typography, layered pink color architecture, and interactive WebGL craft.

## Positioning

"The Pink Standard": unapologetically confident, high-fashion editorial, feminine yet commanding, seamlessly bridging visionary art direction with production-grade engineering.

## Operating Context

Evaluated on desktop monitors, laptops, and mobile screens by aesthetic-conscious industry leaders seeking distinct taste, typographic mastery, and technological execution.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Integrated Originkit "Glimmer Vale" WebGL backdrop with custom pink-standard atmosphere tuning.
- 8 structured editorial sections (Hero, About, Experience, Skills, Selected Work, Education, Personal Philosophy, Contact).
- Interactive CV modal/drawer and resume download capability.
- Micro-interactions, interactive preview modals, custom grain/noise, copy-to-clipboard email, audio/tactile feedback.
- Strict anti-AI-slop design: no generic card grids, no standard bootstrap layouts, no flat boring pinks.

## Brand Commitments

- Palette: Blush (#FCE4EC), Powder Pink (#F4B8CD), Rose (#E88BAF), Hot Pink (#F0529B), Deep Raspberry (#B83270), Burgundy (#591D3B), Soft Ivory (#FFF9F7), Near Black (#21171D).
- Typographic voice: Expressive editorial serif paired with crisp modern geometric sans and editorial numerals.
- Tone: High-fashion editorial, confident, luminous, deeply crafted.

## Evidence on Hand

- Glimmer Vale WebGL component verified and installed via Originkit CLI (`components/originkit/ui/glimmer-vale.tsx`).
- Detailed master specification of Anjali's creative vision, palette, typography, and section breakdown.

## Product Principles

1. **Editorial Authority**: Every viewport must feel art-directed like a luxury monograph or editorial magazine cover.
2. **Pink With Depth**: Layered, tonal, dimensional pinks with soft ivory breathing room and deep burgundy ground.
3. **Restrained Motion & Sensorial Touch**: Smooth, cinematic interactions that elevate content without distracting.
4. **First-Class Mobile Craft**: Fluid typography, generous touch targets, and balanced composition on all screens.
